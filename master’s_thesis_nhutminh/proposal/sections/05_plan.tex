\subsection{Feasibility assessment and Risk mitigation Strategy}

The plan looks feasible within 12 weeks. The model is trained and validated over three seeds, the
backend already runs in Docker with full parity, inference needs no GPU, and every tool in the stack
is open source or has a free tier. The main external cost is GPU time: one training run takes 6.8--8.0
hours on a rented V100, and the plan needs about four more runs (one retrain on the new split and
the ablation arms). The official defense date has not been announced; the schedule will be adjusted
when it is. Table~\ref{tab:risks} lists the identified risks.

\begin{table}[tbp]
\centering
\caption{Risk register. L/I = likelihood/impact (H = high, M = medium, L = low).}
\label{tab:risks}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}P{22mm} L L@{}}

Risk Category & Identified Threat Description & Actionable Mitigation Strategy \\

\textbf{Technical Risk} (L/I: H/H) & The current checkpoint saw tracks that would form the sealed test set & Retrain once (seed 42, $\approx$7 GPU-hours) on the new split; use the sealed set exactly once \\
\textbf{Technical Risk} (L/I: M/M) & CPU latency above 1\,s on the demo host & Measure on Linux (0.19--0.27\,s natively); tune threads; fall back to J1 ($4.17\times$ fewer operations) \\
\textbf{Domain Risk} (L/I: H/H) & Parking lots in Vietnam use Vietnamese plates, which the model has not been trained on & State the Brazilian domain in the interface and the thesis; test 10 Vietnamese plates qualitatively; keep the layout rules configurable so a model trained on Vietnamese data can replace the current one \\
\textbf{Data and Legal Risk} (L/I: H/H) & Dataset images cannot be shown publicly & Store no images; demo with own or openly licensed samples; dataset images only in the thesis and defense \\
\textbf{Resource Risk} (L/I: M/M) & Limited GPU budget & One seed per ablation arm; reuse existing runs \\
\textbf{Schedule Risk} (L/I: M/H) & UI scope grows; defense date not yet fixed & Freeze the UI at three screens after W8; MoSCoW priorities (Must have, Should have, Could have, Won't have); re-plan when the date is set \\
\textbf{Security Risk} (L/I: M/H) & The API has no accounts of its own & Expose it only behind Cloudflare Access; bind the database to localhost; scan images in CI \\
\textbf{Operational Risk} (L/I: L/H) & Loss of reviewer corrections during the user study & Backups every 6\,h kept 7 days, manual dumps before and after sessions; restore tested \\
